Absurd on the surface, exact underneath. Giant headlines can joke. Controls, consent, privacy, and payment copy must be literal.

The participant runtime is the signed-in `/app` plus `extension/`. Landing and `/specimens` are not authorization to run traffic. `$AFK` ledger rows are placeholders (`placeholder_only`) and are not withdrawable. AFKMAXX is not endorsed by Robinhood.
